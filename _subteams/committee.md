# Committee

The **Committee** keeps FSAI moving as one team. We set priorities for the year, manage resources, coordinate subteams, and make sure the technical work has the support it needs to reach competition.

---

## Focus Areas

* **Team strategy**: Setting goals, timelines, and priorities for the academic year.
* **Finance & resources**: Managing budgets, purchasing, and sponsorship planning.
* **Operations**: Coordinating meetings, recruitment, events, documentation, and competition logistics.
* **Cross-team alignment**: Keeping technical and non-technical work connected across all subteams.

---

## Practices

* **Clear communication**: Making sure everyone knows what is happening, who owns what, and what needs attention.
* **Practical planning**: Turning broad goals into achievable milestones.
* **Team support**: Helping subteams remove blockers and get the resources they need.
