# 2026 Highlight

It was the first morning of Formula Student 2026. The sun was out, the roar of the cars on the track was deafening, and the air smelt of asphalt. And before FSAI registration was even open, we'd arrived - we were back, and we were ready for our second year. 

## Travel

Over 2026, FSAI's autonomous driving improved a lot. Unfortunately this was not true for human driving, where we went from two drivers to one, despite three more people coming to competition.

The atmosphere at Silverstone took some getting used to. You're constantly deafened by cars screaming around the track, there's a smell of oil and rubber everywhere, and no one has time for any nonsense.

## DDT: Scrutineering

Our first objective was to pass DDT scrutineering. Even though we'd already tested on the car, getting everything to run properly was difficult. 

The coding tent was where we did most of our work over the first couple of days. We were happy to get the table next to Bath FSAI, as they'd been fantastic allies to us throughout the year - they had many more years of experience than us, but were nevertheless always happy to give us advice and show us how to do things.

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; grid-auto-rows: 150px; gap: 10px; margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 60px;">

It was interesting to see all the different teams at their tables - some had only a couple of members, some wheeled in PCs on shopping trolleys or skateboards, and some (including Bristol's largest university) didn't show up at all...  

Perhaps the most frustrating challenge was the ADS-DVs USB hub. We'd been warned that it was unreliable, but this was an understatement. It in fact never worked. It was with a heavy heart that we admitted, after days of trying to get the inbuilt camera working, that perhaps they had forgotten to plug it in.
