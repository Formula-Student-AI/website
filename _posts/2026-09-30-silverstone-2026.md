# 2026 Highlight

It was the first morning of Formula Student 2026. The sun was out, the roar of the cars on the track was deafening, and the air smelt of asphalt. And before FSAI registration was even open, we'd arrived - we were back, and we were ready for our second year.

I wasn't part of the society at the 2025 competition, so this was my first time. Nevertheless, I'd heard tales about the 2025 campsite - rain, cold showers, a lack of plug sockets, and no easy way to get food. Luckily, my team members had learned from the experience, and the AirBnB we booked was warm, dry, equipped with a kitchen and good WiFi, and somehow cheaper than the extortionate campsite slots.

We arrived two days early, having already tested on the ADS-DV, and with somewhere to stay that was 

As we walked through Silverstone Circuit, deafened by the racecars screaming around the track and with the smell of asphalt in the air, we knew that we'd get the ADS-DV around those cones, and no one was going to stop us (except maybe the judges, if our laps took too long).
