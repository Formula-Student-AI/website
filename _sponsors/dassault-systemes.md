Dassault Systèmes is a French software company behind SOLIDWORKS, CATIA and the 3DEXPERIENCE platform. Engineers around the world use its tools to design, simulate and manufacture everything from cars and aircraft to everyday products.

As a Bronze Sponsor, Dassault Systèmes provides our team with 3DEXPERIENCE platform roles for design (CATIA), simulation (SIMULIA) and data management (ENOVIA). We use them to design and simulate our first car, the suite’s excellent PDM allows smooth cross functional collaboration.
