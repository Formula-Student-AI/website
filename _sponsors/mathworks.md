MathWorks is the leading developer of mathematical computing software, best known for MATLAB and Simulink, which millions of engineers and scientists use to analyse data, develop algorithms and model systems. Its tools are used across automotive, aerospace and motorsport to design and test everything from control systems to autonomous vehicles.

As a Bronze Sponsor, MathWorks provides us with MATLAB, Simulink and Simscape, along with engineering support, technical reviews and workshops for our members. We will use them to build our digital twin, a virtual model of our first car that lets us simulate its powertrain, vehicle dynamics and autonomous control systems and check our designs & validate before we build them.
