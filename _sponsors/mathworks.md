MathWorks is the leading developer of mathematical computing software, best known for MATLAB and Simulink, which millions of engineers use to analyse data and model systems across automotive, aerospace and motorsport.

As a Bronze Sponsor, MathWorks provides us with MATLAB, Simulink and Simscape, plus engineering support, technical reviews and workshops. We will use them to build our digital twin, simulating and validating our first car before we build it.
