Motion Applied, formerly McLaren Applied, is a global leader in motorsport electronics and software, with over 35 years of Formula One heritage. Its control units, sensors and ATLAS data analysis software are used across the world’s most prestigious racing series, and its technology also serves the automotive, aerospace, defence and transport industries.

As a Gold Sponsor, Motion Applied provides us with access to its data acquisition software and Simulink models. We have already put the Simulink models to work in our early development, and this season we will be using them extensively as we design, test and validate our first car.
