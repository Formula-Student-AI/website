Motion Applied, formerly McLaren Applied, is a global leader in motorsport electronics and software, with over 35 years of Formula One heritage. Its control units, sensors and ATLAS software are used across the world’s top racing series and beyond.

As a Gold Sponsor, Motion Applied provides us with its data acquisition software and Simulink models, which we are already using to design, test and validate our first car.
