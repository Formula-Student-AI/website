Harness Design is full CAD software for wiring harness design, available online or as a desktop app. It brings schematics, layouts, pinouts and wiring tables together in one place.

As a Bronze Sponsor, Harness Design provides us with Pro access to its software. We will use it to design every wiring harness on our first car and produce the pinouts, wiring tables and drawings we need to build them.
