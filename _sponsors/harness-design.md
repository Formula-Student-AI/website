Harness Design is full CAD software for wiring harness design, available online or as a desktop app. It brings schematics, layouts, pinouts and wiring tables together in one place, with built-in validation and team collaboration, so complex harnesses are quicker to design, check and build.

As a Bronze Sponsor, Harness Design provides us with Pro access to its software. We will use it to design the wiring harnesses on our first car, from the tractive system and low-voltage electronics to our autonomous sensors. It will produce the pinouts, wiring tables and formboard drawings we need to build them.
