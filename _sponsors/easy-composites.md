Easy Composites is a UK manufacturer and supplier of composite materials, equipment and tooling, from carbon fibre and resins to vacuum bagging supplies. It also publishes a large library of free composites tutorials.

As a Bronze Sponsor, Easy Composites provides us with discounted materials and technical support for our composite parts, including the bodywork and accumulator enclosure.
