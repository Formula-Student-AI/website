Easy Composites is a UK manufacturer and supplier of composite materials, equipment and tooling, from carbon fibre and resins to moulding and vacuum bagging supplies. Alongside its products, it publishes a large library of free tutorials that help engineers and makers produce professional-quality composite parts.

As a Bronze Sponsor, Easy Composites provides us with discounted materials and technical support. We will use them to make the composite parts on our first car, including the bodywork and nose cone, the accumulator enclosure and our sensor mounts.
