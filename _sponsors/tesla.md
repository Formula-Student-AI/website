Tesla is one of the world’s leading electric vehicle and clean energy companies. Its Formula Student programme supports student teams with battery hardware and engineering expertise.

As a Gold Sponsor, Tesla provides us with a battery management system (BMS) and engineering support for our powertrain. The BMS will monitor every cell in our accumulator to keep the pack safe.
