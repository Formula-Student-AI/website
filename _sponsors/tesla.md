Tesla is one of the world's leading electric vehicle and clean energy companies, designing and building EVs, battery packs and energy storage systems. Through its Formula Student programme, it supports student teams building electric race cars with battery hardware and engineering expertise.

As a Gold Sponsor, Tesla provides us with a battery management system (BMS) built on Texas Instruments chips, along with engineering support for our powertrain. The BMS will monitor every cell in our accumulator, keeping the pack safe and healthy as we design, test and race our first car.
